How to use ReviewRight

Step 1 — Employee Details: Enter the employee's role, time in position, and current engagement profile. This calibrates the tone of the review.

Step 2 — Rating & Objectives: Select the performance rating and list formal objectives with met/not met status.

Step 3 — Achievements & Development: Add specific, evidenced achievements and development areas. More detail = stronger review.

Step 4 — Context & Generate: Flag any contextual factors (leave, personal circumstances), promotion consideration, and next period expectations.

Manager Checklist: After generation, you receive a private checklist flagging what's strong, what's weak, and what needs fixing before submission.

Reviews are generated using AI and should be reviewed by the manager before submission.
